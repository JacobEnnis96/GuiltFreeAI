How It Works

The services offered by this website are free forever for unlimited usage with no strings attached. No datacenters are used and none of your data is saved or tracked in any way.

When you choose an AI model (low, medium, high, or high-multimodal), the model itself is downloaded to your computer through your internet browser. This may a take a while depending on your internet speed, current internet traffic, and the model you chose, but it only has to happen once for each model.

This downloaded AI model then uses the processing power of your computer to run and process information. The more powerful your computer is, the faster the AI will respond and operate. For the best results, choose the AI model that offers the best answers at a speed that you find reasonable.

Because these models are running entirely on your computer, anything that you send to the AI including documents, images, and text, stays only on your machine and no one else can ever see it.

The AI models here may not be as intelligent or as fast for all use cases as those offered by the major AI providers, but they're free, they cause no harm, and they will continue to exist and work even if the datacenters go down.

This website is equal parts useful tool and proof of concept. Before long, the average person will not need to use AI models hosted by datacenters, and most AI chat for individual people will run on each person's computer or phone. AI datacenters are likely to be used almost entirely by research institutes and corporations for heavier use cases like software development and scientific research.

Enjoy, and use these little AI models for whatever you wish. -Jacob, AI Developer and designer of GuiltFreeAI.
